Back office

Production, purchasing, distribution, in-flight, inventory, accounts, reports, settings
Making and sending food
Back office sign in → Production → Distribution → Outlet receives delivery
Production records what was made and the raw materials used. Distribution sends it to an outlet, and the outlet counts it on arrival.
Buying and stock
Purchasing → Approvals → Notifications → Receive stock → Inventory
A request is approved by someone other than the requester. Received stock goes into inventory.
In-flight catering and money
In-flight catering → Airline weekly invoice
Deliveries to airlines feed a weekly invoice, which posts to the main account.
The books
Accounts overview → Chart of accounts → Journals → General ledger → Trial balance → Financial statements
Every module posts to Accounts. A manual journal is approved by someone other than its preparer. The chart of accounts sets which accounts exist.
Checks and month end
Receivables and payables → Payments → Bank reconciliation → Close a period
Ageing, payments and the bank are checked against the ledger before a period is closed. A closed period is reopened only with a reason.

Things Remembered click-through demo
Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Safari. No server needed. Best on a wide screen. Sample data only.
Works fully offline. The Inter font is bundled in the fonts folder (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

Sign-in accepts any username and password. Demo supervisor PIN: 1234.
To see the barcode scan box, switch on Barcode scanning for MM2 outlet in Settings, Sales rules (barcode 6001234500012 adds bottled water).

Some things are remembered in the browser so the screens agree with each other: menu badges (Notifications, Approvals), the approvals you decide, held sales, cash outs, recalled baskets, and changes in Settings, Sales rules and Price rates. Most other screens start fresh each time you open them.
To reset: use "Reset demo data" on index.html, or open the demo in a private window.
Nothing is downloaded when you use Export. The screen only shows what would be prepared.
Airline invoice: choose Edit invoice (while it is a Draft) to set your logo, business details, TIN, bank details, who it is billed to, PO number, dates, payment terms, currency, items, VAT, discount and notes. Business details are saved for every invoice. Download PDF opens the browser print window: choose Save as PDF to get a real file named after the invoice number. The sample TIN, address and bank account are made up. Nigerian invoice fields were checked against vendor guides, not the Nigeria Tax Act itself, so confirm the required fields with your tax adviser.

Navigation: Accounts, Settings and Reports each have a short row of groups at the top. Inside a group, a quieter second row switches between its pages.
Accounts is built around what needs doing: the Overview lists only unfinished month-end work, Journals shows waiting approvals first, and clicking an account row opens that account's ledger.
Before and after screenshots, with the reasons for each change, are in the ux-review folder (open ux-review/index.html).

For whoever edits the demo
The back office shares one stylesheet (app.css), one script for list screens (list-engine.js), and one definition of the menu and tabs (ux-review/tools/sync.py). Change those, not individual pages.
After changing navigation or the list-screen template, run:  python3 ux-review/tools/sync.py
The Cash Point app has its own stylesheet (pos.css).
Conventions: ux-review/COMPONENTS.md. What was found and changed, screen by screen: ux-review/AUDIT.md. Tests (real clicks and typing in headless Chrome): ux-review/tools/README.txt.
Remembered in the browser as well (Operations): purchase requests and their approvals, dispatches, received stock. Use "Reset demo data" on the start page to clear them.

Screen sizes: the back office works from phone to wide desktop. The Cash Point is for tablets and tills: use it at 768px wide or more (the sale screen scrolls sideways below 700px).
